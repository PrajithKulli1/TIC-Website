Who's behind this

Founder-led. Direct by design.

TIC is founder-led by Prajith Gowri Shankar, a Computer Science student at NC State. Today, TIC is intentionally small: when you work with us, you work directly with the person responsible for scoping and building your project.

As projects grow beyond what one person can handle well, TIC brings in trusted collaborators on a project-by-project basis — disclosed upfront and selected for the work at hand.

We're early, and we're transparent about that. That means fewer layers, direct communication, and personal accountability for what gets shipped.
